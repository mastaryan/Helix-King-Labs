GHK-Cu Research Guide

GHK-Cu is a copper-binding tripeptide (glycyl-histidyl-lysine) studied in skin, collagen, and gene-expression models.

What it is

GHK-Cu pairs the tripeptide GHK with a copper(II) ion, forming a small complex with high affinity for copper. It occurs naturally in human plasma and has one of the longest publication histories of any research peptide, spanning dermatological, wound-healing, and gene-modulation literature.

How it's studied

Preclinical and cosmetic-science literature investigates GHK-Cu in collagen synthesis, dermal remodeling, and antioxidant-pathway models. Its distinctive blue color in solution comes from the copper complex — a useful visual identity check at the bench.

What to look for

  • Purity ≥99% by HPLC with chromatogram.
  • Copper content — the COA should confirm the Cu complex, not just the peptide.
  • Blue tint on reconstitution — a quick sanity check (not a substitute for the COA).
  • Lot-matched COA with your vial's HKL- code.

Storage

Lyophilized: −20°C sealed, away from light (copper complexes are light-sensitive). See our storage guide.
