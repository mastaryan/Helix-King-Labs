Research Peptides FAQ

Straight answers about our products, testing, ordering, and policies.

Products & Testing

What are research peptides?

Research peptides are short chains of amino acids supplied strictly as laboratory research materials. Ours ship in labeled vials for in-vitro research use only — not for human or animal use. Start with our introduction to research peptides.

Do your products come with a certificate of analysis?

Yes. Every lot is third-party tested and ships with a certificate of analysis covering identity, purity (HPLC), endotoxin, sterility, heavy metals, residual solvents, and TFA content. Browse lot certificates or read how to read a COA.

How do I verify the lot on my vial?

Each vial carries a lot code starting with HKL-. Look it up on our certificates page to pull the exact COA for that lot.

How should peptides be stored?

Lyophilized peptides store best frozen at −20°C, sealed with desiccant and kept away from light. Full details in our peptide storage guide.
