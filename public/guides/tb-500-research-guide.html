TB-500 Research Guide

TB-500 is the synthetic 43-amino-acid fragment of thymosin beta-4, studied in cell migration and tissue repair models.

What it is

Thymosin beta-4 is a naturally occurring 43-amino-acid peptide found in nearly all mammalian cells. TB-500 is its synthetic research fragment, notable for the actin-binding domain that preclinical literature associates with cell migration and differentiation pathways.

How it's studied

Published work explores TB-500 in wound-healing, cardiac, corneal, and musculoskeletal models. It is frequently studied alongside BPC-157 — the combination appears across preclinical repair literature, which is why both are stocked together.

What to look for

  • Purity ≥99% by HPLC — TB-500's length (43 AA) makes synthesis impurities more likely; the chromatogram matters.
  • Identity by mass spec — theoretical mass ~4963 Da.
  • Endotoxin pass — relevant for any cell-culture work.
  • Lot-matched COA with your vial's HKL- code.

Storage

Lyophilized: −20°C sealed. Reconstituted: 2–8°C, ~30 days. Full details in our storage guide.
