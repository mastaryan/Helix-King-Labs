Tirzepatide Research Guide

Tirzepatide is a 39-amino-acid dual-agonist peptide (GIP / GLP-1 receptors) widely studied in metabolic research.

What it is

Tirzepatide is a synthetic peptide that activates both the GIP and GLP-1 receptors. Its sequence incorporates a C20 fatty diacid moiety that extends half-life, and it has one of the largest clinical trial programs of any peptide in its class.

How it's studied

Beyond the clinical literature, research materials are used in receptor pharmacology, analytical method development, and stability studies. It is the most referenced compound in the current metabolic-peptide literature, which also makes it the most counterfeited — COA scrutiny matters more here than anywhere.

What to look for

  • Purity ≥99% by HPLC with chromatogram — demand it; this compound attracts low-grade copies.
  • Identity by mass spec — theoretical mass ~4813 Da.
  • Full panel — endotoxin, sterility, metals, solvents, TFA. No shortcuts.
  • Lot-matched COA with your vial's HKL- code, from a named accredited lab.

Storage

Lyophilized: −20°C sealed. Reconstituted: 2–8°C. See our storage guide.
