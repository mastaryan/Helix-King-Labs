BPC-157 Research Guide

BPC-157 is a 15-amino-acid peptide fragment studied in tissue repair and gastrointestinal research models.

What it is

BPC-157 (Body Protection Compound-157) is a pentadecapeptide derived from a sequence found in human gastric juice protein. Its 15-amino-acid chain (Gly-Glu-Pro-Pro-Pro-Gly-Lys-Pro-Ala-Asp-Asp-Ala-Gly-Leu-Val) has made it one of the most widely referenced peptides in preclinical repair-pathway literature.

How it's studied

Published preclinical work investigates BPC-157 in tendon, ligament, muscle, and GI tract models, with proposed mechanisms involving growth factor modulation and nitric oxide pathways. All findings to date are preclinical — it remains strictly a research compound.

What to look for

  • Purity ≥99% by HPLC with a visible chromatogram on the COA.
  • Correct identity by mass spec — 1419.5 Da theoretical monoisotopic mass.
  • Low TFA — counter-ion content under 1%.
  • Lot-matched COA — the report must carry your vial's HKL- lot code.

Storage

Lyophilized: −20°C sealed, 24+ months. Reconstituted: 2–8°C, ~30 days. See our storage guide.
