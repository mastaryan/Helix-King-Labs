Eloralintide Research Guide

Eloralintide ("Elora" in press coverage) is Eli Lilly's investigational once-weekly selective amylin receptor agonist — the compound behind the EloraTZP combination data presented at EASD 2026.

What it is

Amylin is a hormone co-secreted with insulin from pancreatic beta cells. It regulates blood sugar and promotes satiety through its own receptor pathway — distinct from GLP-1 and GIP. Eloralintide is a long-acting synthetic amylin analog designed for once-weekly administration, currently in clinical development and not approved or commercially available.

Why researchers are watching

At the EASD 2026 annual meeting (Milan, Sept 28–Oct 2), Lilly presented phase 2b data on EloraTZP — eloralintide combined with tirzepatide — in 367 adults with obesity or overweight plus type 2 diabetes, a population where large sustained weight loss has historically been hard to achieve. The combination targets three nutrient-stimulated hormone pathways at once: amylin, GIP, and GLP-1.

The EloraTZP phase 2b results (48 weeks)

  • Highest combo dose (Elora 9 mg + tirzepatide 15 mg): mean 23.3% weight loss (24.6 kg), HbA1c down 2.9%.
  • Lowest combo dose (Elora 3 mg + tirzepatide 5 mg): mean 13.2% weight loss, HbA1c down 2.2%.
  • Tirzepatide 15 mg alone: 14.8% weight loss.
  • Eloralintide alone: 8.2–12.3% across doses.
  • Placebo: 3.0%.

All four combination regimens met the primary and secondary endpoints. The standout number: the top combo dose beat tirzepatide monotherapy by nearly 9 percentage points.

The tradeoff

Gastrointestinal adverse effects were the most common — generally mild to moderate and concentrated during dose escalation — but they occurred more often with the combination, and discontinuation rates ran 10.8–27.0% on EloraTZP versus 2.9% for tirzepatide alone. Efficacy versus tolerability is the open question clinicians are weighing.

What to look for in research material

  • Purity ≥99% by HPLC with chromatogram — investigational compounds attract early low-grade copies.
  • Identity by mass spec — confirm the expected molecular weight for the specific salt form.
  • Full panel — endotoxin, sterility, metals, solvents. No shortcuts.
  • Lot-matched COA from a named accredited lab. See our guide to reading a COA.
